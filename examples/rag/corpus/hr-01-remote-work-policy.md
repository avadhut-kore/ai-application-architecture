# Remote Work and Working Hours Policy

## 1. Core Working Hours
To facilitate cross-timezone collaboration, all full-time employees must remain synchronously reachable during core business hours between **10:00 AM and 3:00 PM** local time in their designated home office region.

## 2. Equipment and Home Office Stipend
Employees in approved remote arrangements are eligible for a one-time setup allowance of **$500** for home ergonomics and office equipment. Enterprise laptops and peripherals remain corporate property and must be returned within 14 days of employment separation.

## 3. Cross-Border Work Travel
Employees requesting to work temporarily outside their designated country of residence must submit an international work request at least **14 calendar days** prior to travel to assess international tax and legal obligations.
